Nothing is drawn that the words do not name, and nothing is named that has no emoji. The tests rebuild the traits from the sentence alone and from the emoji alone and check both against the original, on 120,000 pets. Four extra slots over v3 take this from 46 million identities to 2,305,071,267,840, so the three-way rule no longer costs security. Forging one that survives a full reading takes about 115 days on one core, or 16 minutes on hardware ten thousand times faster, which is why the seal is still here: with it, 71 years even at that rate. A wrong pet means stop. A right pet is still not proof, so check the address too.
