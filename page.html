wordpet

Type two or three words. The same words always hatch the exact same pet, down to the pixel, so a person can recognise their own at a glance and spot a stranger's.

This is version 1. v2 names every part out loud, and v3 makes picture, sentence and emoji agree, and v5 is the current one, with a new look and 48 animals.

Fingerprint
At avatar size

    Hatch one of these

    The words are lowercased and stripped to letters, digits and single dashes, so Blue Panda, blue--panda and BLUE_PANDA are one pet. That string is hashed to a 128-bit fingerprint, which seeds every choice below: hue, body, ears, eyes, mouth, hat, arms, held item, legs, tail, worn item, coat pattern and how it moves. Nothing reads the clock, the locale or the machine, so the same words draw the same pixels everywhere.
